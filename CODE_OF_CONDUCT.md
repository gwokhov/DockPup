# Community Guidelines

Help make DockPup a respectful place to collaborate:

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, and personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed
to protect the community. These guidelines apply to project issues, pull
requests, and discussions.

Report concerns privately through a contact channel listed by a repository
maintainer. If none is available, ask for a private channel without posting
personal details or naming other people in a public report.
